## Disposition vocabulary

- **DO** — use in the canonical run
- **ALT** — explain a viable alternate strategy or solution
- **SHOW** — demonstrate from a disposable checkpoint or separate recording
- **SAY** — narrate without performing it
- **SKIP** — intentionally omit, with a reason

## Final integration gate

- [ ] Every applicable mechanics-catalog finding has DO/ALT/SHOW/SAY/SKIP disposition.
- [ ] Every unusual-design item has a purpose disposition before final route synthesis, including unused alternates and recovery functions.
- [ ] Gameplay-function hypotheses and documented authorial intent are labeled separately.
- [ ] Every SKIP has a reason.
- [ ] Every step records hidden time/update/hazard costs where applicable.
- [ ] Required health/resources/weight/equipment/state are explicit.
- [ ] Every irreversible action has preparation and failure consequences.
- [ ] Every risky strategy has retry or recovery guidance.
- [ ] Every relocated/persistent threat includes later-crossing consequences.
- [ ] Important alternate solutions are represented.
- [ ] Useful, dangerous, and misleading HELP/manual advice appears proactively.
- [ ] Original and repaired behavior are never mixed silently.
- [ ] Checkpoint demonstrations cannot damage the canonical winning state.
- [ ] Concise omissions remain covered by scenic notes or separate demonstrations.
- [ ] Later discoveries reopened affected purpose hypotheses and route assumptions rather than being appended without integration.
- [ ] The route has been rehearsed against the named edition/configuration.
